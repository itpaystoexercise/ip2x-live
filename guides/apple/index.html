Before you start

What you need

An iPhone

The IP2X app runs here. It starts every workout and uploads your data.

An Apple Watch paired to it

It records your heart rate and wrist motion, and buzzes if your heart rate goes over your limit.

The TestFlight app

Apple's free app for installing test versions. Get it from the App Store on your iPhone.

An invite from us

An email or a link from the IP2X team. Send us the Apple ID email you use on your iPhone so we can add you.

Using an Android phone or a Galaxy Watch? That version is tested separately and isn't on TestFlight. We'll send you its own instructions.
Step one

Accept the invite and install

  1. Install TestFlight

    Get TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone, if you don't already have it.

  2. Open the invite on your iPhone

    Open the email from TestFlight on the iPhone itself and tap View in TestFlight. If we sent you a link, just tap the link.

  3. Accept, then Install

    TestFlight shows IP2X. Tap Accept, then Install.

  4. Check the watch app is on your watch

    The watch app comes with the phone app. If it isn't on your watch after a minute, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and tap Install next to IP2X.

Updates come through TestFlight too. When we release a new build, TestFlight lets you know. Tap Update, then follow the steps in Keep the app up to date, just below.
Read these two before your first launch. Nearly every "it's not working" comes back to one of them.

1Keep the app up to date

Updates come in two sizes, and you'll handle each a little differently.

Small updates · arrive by themselvesMost fixes go straight into the app, with nothing to install. The app downloads them quietly while it's open, and uses them only when it next starts from scratch. Sending it to the background doesn't count. The steps below make that happen.
Big updates · install from TestFlightSome changes, especially to the watch app, need a new install. When one is ready, the app shows a card on its home screen, A newer IP2X is in TestFlight, and TestFlight shows an Update button next to IP2X. Tap the card, or open TestFlight yourself. See the TestFlight steps further down.

For a small update: tap the card

The app looks for small updates when you open it and again each time you come back to it. Once one has downloaded, this card appears at the top of the home screen (never while a session is running). Tap it and the app restarts on the new version:

No card? Give the app a moment in the foreground, or do it by hand:

Open the app, wait ~30 s→Swipe it away in the app switcher→Open it again→New version running ✓
  1. When we tell you there's an update, open IP2X and leave it open for about 30 seconds while it downloads.
  2. Close it completely: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause to see the app switcher, then flick IP2X up and away.
  3. Open it again. That launch runs the new version.
  4. Check you're on it: the app shows its code version on the home screen and at the bottom of Settings. It should match the version we sent you. If it doesn't, do steps 1–3 once more.

For a big update: install it from TestFlight

When a big update is waiting for your phone, the home screen shows this card instead. Tapping it opens TestFlight:

  1. Open the TestFlight app.
  2. Find IP2X and tap Update. The watch app updates along with it, which can take a few minutes.
  3. Open IP2X. If a small update is waiting for the new build, the home screen shows A small update is ready within a minute; tap it. Until then the code version can read untagged. That's expected.
  4. The version number has gone up. Run Check my setup once to confirm the phone and watch still work together, and allow anything the watch asks for.
  5. Updating to 0.3.14? The first launch asks a few questions about you, once, then goes to your home screen. The Health sheet may show once more, with new rows for date of birth, sex, height and weight. Allow it.

Please install big updates soon. Small fixes only reach the newest version, so an old install quietly stops getting them. Tip: in TestFlight, open IP2X and turn on Automatic Updates, and big updates install by themselves.

2Allow every permission, on the phone and the watch

Without these, a session looks fine on screen but records nothing, and the app can't always tell you why. There are three permissions, and they come at different moments:

📱 iPhone · HealthAt your first launch, sometimes before you've even typed your name. Turn on everything, including date of birth, sex, height and weight, then tap Allow.
⌚ Watch · HealthThe first time you open IP2X on the watch. Allow it on your wrist.
⌚ Watch · Motion & FitnessAt your first set or the setup check. Allow it on your wrist.
  1. Say yes to every sheet. If you tap "Don't Allow" by mistake, iOS won't ask again. Turn it on yourself: iPhone Health app → your picture (top right) → Apps → IP2X → turn on all categories.
  2. If the watch asks during a session, allow both prompts on your wrist and keep going. Your phone app closes when you allow Motion & Fitness. That's iOS, not a crash: open IP2X again and it rejoins the session. Afterwards run Check my setup once, so you see Ready to record.
  3. Changing a permission in Settings makes the app restart. That's iOS, not a crash. Just open it again.
  4. Pressed End on the phone but the watch still shows the workout? Press End on the watch as well. We're fixing this.

The phone can't see what you allowed on the watch, so Check my setup is how you find out. Aim for Ready to record.

Step two

First launch

After installing or updating, the app finishes the job itself. Within a minute of opening it, the home screen may show A small update is ready. Tap it and the app restarts on the newest version. Nothing else to do.

  1. Tell us your name

    The first screen says Welcome to IP2X and asks What's your name? Your name is how we label your data, and nothing else.

    Your name is tied to this phone. Once it's set, the app won't let you change it, so your history stays yours. If you ever reinstall, type exactly the same name. If the app says the name is already in use, enter your recovery code.

  2. Tap Let's get set up

    The screen tells you what happens next: your phone asks for health access, we ask a few questions about you, we find your recent data and upload it, we check your setup.

  3. Allow health access

    iOS shows the Health permission sheet. Turn on everything it asks for and tap Allow. The sheet now lists date of birth, sex, height and weight as well: the app reads them so some of the next answers are filled in for you.

    On a brand-new install the sheet can appear before you've typed your name. Allow it, then type your name and carry on. It won't ask twice.

  4. Answer a few questions about you

    Eight questions, one per screen: when you were born (month and year), your sex at birth, your height, your weight, which wrist wears the watch, your dominant hand, how often you train, and whether you take medication that affects your heart rate. The first screen says why: they help us read your heart rate fairly and map your watch's movement. Tap I agree, continue.

    Scroll each wheel to your answer and tap Next; on the last one, tap Save. Every question needs an answer, and Prefer not to say counts as one. Height and weight start in feet and stones; tap the toggle for cm and kg. Anything Health already knew is filled in for you, so just check it. Your age sets your heart-rate limit unless you've set one yourself in Settings.

  5. Open IP2X on your watch and allow both prompts

    The watch asks for permission twice, at different times. It asks for Health the first time you open the watch app. It asks for Motion & Fitness the first time it records motion, which is your first set or the setup check. Allow both on your wrist. Until both are allowed, sessions won't record.

Change or delete your answers any time. Open Settings in the app and find Your answers. Edit walks you through the same screens with your current answers filled in. Delete asks you to tap twice, then removes your answers from the phone and from our server, and the app asks the questions again next time you open it.
If the app restarts after you change a permission, that's iOS, not a crash. iOS restarts an app when you change one of its permissions in Settings. Open IP2X again and carry on. Nothing is lost.

After the setup check, the app shows a short three-card guide and offers a Practice session. Practice runs the whole flow (watch, timer, reps, upload) but never counts. It's a good way to try things out.

Step three

Check my setup

Check my setup runs automatically during onboarding. It's also on the Session screen whenever you want it. It takes about half a minute: it tests the phone's upload, then runs a short session on the watch. Wear the watch while it runs. If the watch asks for Motion & Fitness during the check, tap Allow on your wrist.

What the marks mean

✓Working ?Couldn't confirm from here ✕Needs fixing –Doesn't apply to you

A ? is not a failure. Some things, like whether you granted Health access, iOS never tells an app directly. The check only says ✓ once it has actually seen the data arrive.

The three possible results

Ready to record

Everything that applies to your setup is working. You're good to train.

Mostly ready

Nothing is broken, but something couldn't be confirmed yet. Read the rows marked ?. This is common right after installing, before any health data has come in. Run it again in a few minutes.

Something needs fixing

Sessions may not record properly. Each row marked ✕ says exactly what to do. Do that, then tap Run again.

Step four

Train, and count your reps honestly

  1. Tap an exercise, or a routine

    Train is home. It says Nothing is recording. Tap an exercise to begin. Tap one of the seven tiles below, or Other › to search the full list. Your routines sit above the tiles.

  2. Answer the phone question, then tap Start

    A short sheet asks Record your phone's movement too? Nothing is picked for you, and Start stays greyed out until you answer. Which answer to pick. Then tap Start. The watch starts by itself. You don't need to touch it.

  3. Check how you're wearing the watch

    The exercise screen has a Watch card that says how you're wearing it. If it's wrong, tap Change and set Wrist LeftRight and Crown toward HandElbow. It remembers your answer, so usually you only check it. How to tell. Tap Watch demo ▶ to see the exercise.

  4. Start, do your set, tap Done

    The screen says Start when you are in position. Tap Done after your last rep. Tap Start, do your set with the watch on, then tap Done.

  5. Enter your reps, then Real set or Spoof

    The app asks How many reps did you manage? Enter the true number, including 0. Your honest count is what the system learns from.

    Then it asks Was this a real set? Nothing is chosen for you: tap Real set or Spoof, then Save set. Nearly every set is a real set. When to tap Spoof.

  6. End the session, then leave the app open briefly

    Tap your next exercise, or follow your routine's Next. When you've finished, tap End on the recording bar, then End session, save it. Your data only syncs while the app is open, so keep it open for a few seconds afterwards. You'll see a line like "Recorded 24 minutes of motion and heart rate ✓". That means your data is saved.

Don't start a workout from the watch. A workout started from the watch has no heart-rate limit, so it can't buzz to warn you. It also doesn't count toward your plan. The watch app has no Start button, only the message above. While a session is running, the watch shows your heart rate, your limit, and an End button. If you go over your limit it buzzes and shows EASE OFF.

The seven featured exercises

How you're wearing the watch

Wrist and crown, checked before every session

The same movement looks different to the watch depending on which wrist it's on and which way round it sits. Telling the app lets us line the motion up before we count reps. The crown is the dial on the side of the watch.

Look at your watch with your arm out in front of you. The crown points toward your fingers.

Crown toward: Hand

The crown points back toward your elbow. Common if you wear the watch "upside down" or on the other wrist.

Crown toward: Elbow

Wrist is simply the arm the watch is on: Left or Right.

Swapped mid-session? On the exercise screen, tap Change on the Watch card. The change applies from that exercise on, and next session starts from it.

After every set

Real or Spoof: say what actually happened

We're building a system that can tell a genuine set from a faked one. To learn that, it needs examples of both, each labelled truthfully by the person who did it. A spoof isn't a mistake or a cheat. It's data we want, and you're free to do as many as you like.

Real set

You did the exercise you picked, properly, and the rep count is true. This is almost every set.

Spoof

You deliberately faked it. For example:

  • moving the watch arm through the motion without the effort or the weight
  • doing a different exercise from the one you picked
  • entering more reps than you did

Spoof whenever you like, but always label it Spoof. A faked set saved as a real set teaches the system the wrong thing.

Made a mistake? Tapped the wrong button, or entered a rep count that's clearly wrong? Get in touch with us and tell us roughly when the set was. We'll decide what to do with it. A count that's off by one is fine, no need to tell us.
The one rule that matters most

Count only the reps your watch arm did

The watch can only feel the arm it's on. We're teaching the system to count reps from that wrist's motion, so the number you enter has to describe what that wrist did. A rep done by your other arm leaves no trace on the watch. If you count it anyway, the motion and the number no longer match, and the system learns the wrong thing.

Alternating or one arm at a time (for example alternating bicep curls). You did 8 with each arm.

Enter 8, not 16.

Both arms move together (push-up, squat, sit-up, a two-handed press). The watch arm does every rep.

Enter the reps you did, e.g. 6.

A one-arm set with the non-watch arm. The watch arm stayed still.

Enter 0. Better still, swap the watch over or do that set with your watch arm.

Every session

The phone question

Every session starts with Record your phone's movement too? With the phone in your pocket, IP2X records how it moves, as a second sensor for your legs and hips. Your watch records as usual either way.

Nothing is picked for you, because where your phone is can change from one session to the next. Pick the answer that is true for this session:

Yes, left thigh pocket
Yes, right thigh pocket

The phone is in a trouser or shorts pocket on that thigh.

Yes, on my waistband

The phone is clipped or tucked at your waist.

No, not this time

The phone is on a bench, in a bag, or in your hand. That's a full answer, and the watch still records.

The first time you say Yes, the app explains what it records, then iOS asks to use Motion & Fitness. Allow it. Phone motion is recorded only during a session. Keep the screen facing out, top pointing up.

If you turned Motion & Fitness off, the sheet says so and offers Turn it on in You. You can still answer No, not this time and train.

New in update v0.3.55

Routines: plan a session, then follow it

A routine is the list of exercises you want to do, in order, like Monday legs: Squats, Lunges, Glute bridge. It doesn't hold sets, reps or weights; you decide those on the day. An exercise can be in a routine more than once. A routine holds up to 20 exercises, and you can keep 50 routines.

Make one on my.ip2x.co.uk

  1. Open Routines

    Log in to my.ip2x.co.uk (how). At the top, tap Routines, then New routine.

  2. Add exercises

    Search or pick exercises to add them. Use the arrows to move one up or down, and × to take one out.

  3. Name it and save

    Type a name, like Monday legs, and tap Save routine.

To change a routine, tap it on the Routines page. To delete one, open it, tap Delete, then Tap again to delete. When you have 50, New routine is greyed out until you delete one.

Start it from the app

Your routines appear on Train, above the exercise tiles, the one you used most recently first. Tap one: the start sheet shows its name and exercises. Answer the phone question and tap Start. A routine you make or change on the website shows up the next time you open Train with an internet connection. With no routines yet, Train says Plan routines at my.ip2x.co.uk, or save one after a session.

During the session

After you save each set, a card shows where you are, like 2 of 3 · Monday legs, and what comes next.

Next: Lunges

Opens the next exercise. Nothing records until you tap Start.

Skip

Moves past the next exercise without doing it.

Change exercise

Do something else in its place. The list opens with Instead of Lunges, pick any exercise. Pick one, or tap Back to routine.

‹ Train

For an extra exercise. The routine waits, and a strip on Train, like Monday legs · Next: Glute bridge ›, takes you back.

Tap the 2 of 3 line to see the whole routine: done, skipped, and still to do. After the last exercise the card shows End session. There's no button for another set of the same exercise right away; to repeat one, use Change exercise or put it in the routine twice.

After the session

You trained without a routine

Save this as a routine? Type a name and tap Save, or Not now. The exercises are kept in the order you did them; several sets of one exercise in a row count once.

You followed a routine exactly

Nothing to answer.

You changed a routine

The app says You changed Monday legs. Tap Update Monday legs, Save as a new routine, or Not now.

The app can save and update routines, but renaming, reordering and deleting happen on the website. No signal at the gym? Train still shows the routines your phone last saw, and a routine saved without a connection is sent when you're back online.

Your account

Your name, recovery code and website

IP2X has no email and no password. Your name is your account, and your phone keeps a hidden key that proves the name is yours. Your workouts are saved on our server under that name, so they aren't lost if the app is.

Save your recovery code

The recovery code gets your name back if your phone ever loses its hidden key.

  1. Update the app

    When the home screen shows A small update is ready, tap it. You need update v0.3.40 or later.

  2. Save the code when it appears

    A Save your recovery code card appears once. Tap Save this code and keep it in Notes, a password manager, or a screenshot. Then tap I've saved it.

  3. Find it again any time

    Go to You → Settings → Recovery code and tap Show recovery code.

A code is 12 letters and numbers in three groups, like ABCD-EFGH-JKLM (an example). Capitals don't matter, and you can type it with or without the dashes. Treat it like a password.

If you delete the app or change phone

Same iPhone, or a new one restored from an encrypted backup

Nothing to do. Type your same name when you set up again; your iPhone kept the hidden key.

A new phone set up fresh, or a lost or reset phone

Type your name. The app says it's already in use and asks for your recovery code. Enter it and your name and history come back. You then get a new code to save; the old one stops working.

Lost the code as well

Tell us. Once we've confirmed it's you, we'll help you get your name back.

Before you delete the app, open it once with an internet connection. Anything recorded but not yet uploaded lives only on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

See your data on my.ip2x.co.uk

Look at your sessions, each one's heart-rate curve, and download everything we hold about you. There's no password: the app gives you a code, the way a banking app does. You need update v0.3.44 or later.

  1. Get a code in the app

    Go to You → Log in to the website. You'll see a 6-digit code. It works once and lasts 2 minutes; tap Get a new code if it runs out.

  2. Log in on the website

    Go to my.ip2x.co.uk (or tap Log in at the top of ip2x.co.uk) and type your name and the code.

  3. Sign out when you're done

    You stay logged in for 8 hours. On a shared computer, sign out on the website. Left yourself logged in somewhere? In the app, tap Sign out of the website everywhere.

Only type a login code on my.ip2x.co.uk, and only one you just got from your own app. We'll never ask you for one.

The website is also where you plan your routines.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time: sessions, heart-rate and motion readings, your health answers and your name. Log in to my.ip2x.co.uk, open your account and ask us to delete your data. A person on the team handles it by hand, within a month, as UK data protection law requires. Backup copies delete themselves within 14 days after that. Once your data is gone your name goes too, so you won't be able to log in afterwards.

Delete the app from your phone too. If IP2X is still installed, it keeps saving new workouts under your name after we've deleted your data.
When things go sideways

If something's off

The app sits on "Starting…" after the first launch

That's the old version packed into the install, doing a long first read. Close the app completely and open it again. Nothing is lost.

"Watch did not confirm the session"

Usually a watch permission hasn't been allowed yet. Open IP2X on the watch and allow the Health sheet. Do one warm-up set with your wrist up so the Motion & Fitness prompt appears, then allow it. Then run Check my setup.

"Motion data isn't reaching the phone — keep the watch close."

Keep your phone near you while you train. If it keeps happening, restart the watch (hold the side button, drag Power Off, then hold it again to turn it back on) and run the check.

The session summary says no motion was recorded

Restart the watch app, run Check my setup, and tell us. A watch that has stopped sending data doesn't show any error, and a restart almost always fixes it.

I closed the app, or it restarted, in the middle of a session

You don't lose anything. Your logged sets are already saved, and the watch keeps recording through the gap. When you reopen the app, it picks the watch back up by itself. Anything not uploaded yet goes up the next time the app is open and online.

My workout isn't showing up

Data only syncs while the app is open. Open IP2X after your session and leave it open for a few seconds.

Looking in the Fitness app (or Health → Workouts)? Your IP2X session isn't listed under the name IP2X. Look for Functional Strength Training; tap it and you'll see IP2X as the source.

The Health sheet popped up before I'd typed my name

A timing quirk on a brand-new install. Allow it, then type your name and carry on. It won't ask again.

I want to change one of my answers

Open Settings, find Your answers and tap Edit. Change what you need and tap Save on the last screen.

The app won't let me change my name

That's deliberate: your name is tied to this phone so your history stays yours. Moving to a new phone? Use your recovery code. If you need the name itself changed, tell us.

Tell us anything, any time. Use Report a problem at the bottom of the Session screen. It sends us your note along with the details we need to look into it.
